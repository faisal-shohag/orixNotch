## Product name
OrixNotch

## Description
OrixNotch — Your Screen, Smarter.

A dynamic-island style toolbox for Windows. A small pill sits at the top-center of your screen — hover it, click it, or press Ctrl+Alt+N and it springs open into a panel of everyday tools: Now Playing, File Shelf, Clipboard history, Quick Note, To-Dos, Pomodoro timer, Calendar, Weather, System Monitor, Stocks, Unit Converter, and Emoji picker.

Smoother • Faster • Smarter. Everything is stored locally on your PC; network is used only for weather, stocks, lyrics, and optional AI features, and only while those tools are in use.

- Dynamic-island pill UI with spring animations
- Now Playing controls for Spotify, browsers & media apps
- File Shelf — park files and text, drag them back out
- Searchable clipboard history (text, links, images, files)
- Quick Note auto-saving scratchpad + To-Dos
- Pomodoro focus timer with pill countdown
- Weather, Calendar, Stocks, System Monitor & more
